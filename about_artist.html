About Me

Hello, my name is Trezha and I go by AesthetixVB online. I'm a 25 year old Filipino-American artist and aspiring game developer. I love playing video games, I love animation, I loved creating games/worlds. I have a dedicated Game Journal that I keep for myself. It details my ideas, plans, designs for games I want to make. But you can learn more of that here.

Web development is also something that interests me a lot. I vividly remember what it was like to browse the internet before social media sites like Facebook took over and changed the entire social webscape. I always thought people having personal websites was super cool and through my rediscovery of this love in my adulthood, I now take the leap into learning whatever I can in order to develop this website as a personal project of mine.
