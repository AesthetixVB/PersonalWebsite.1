About the brand

AesthetixVB is my online brand and username that I go by. Aesthetix derives from the word Aesthetic. "Concerned with beauty or the appreciation of beauty" relates to how I feel about things in life. I like bringing attention to the small things and details of life and living.

VB stands for Von Bargen, my last name passed down by my late father (RIP Dad 2019).
