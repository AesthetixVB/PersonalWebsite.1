About the Site

I created this website as a creative outlet and hub for my artwork and development projects. I also wanted to contribute to the online movement and community moving away from big social media platforms. By developing this site I get to express myself wholeheartedly, and organize my own chaos into website form. I also hope by slowly contributing to this project overtime, would also improve my HTML and web development skills greatly. Make sure to visit the site neighborhood, part of learning how to do this involves exploring and taking a lot of inspiration, and these sites deserve to be visited as well.

I've been trying to develop a personal website off and on for years now. But as I am writting this, I will declare today (7.18.2026) the very first official day of development. No more are the days of stagnation! I will actually contribute as much as I can.

About the brand

AesthetixVB is my online brand and username that I go by. Aesthetix derives from the word Aesthetic, which is my favorite word of all time. "Concerned with beauty or the appreciation of beauty" represents how I feel about most things in life. Nature, culture, buildings and structures, I like bringing attention to the small things and details of life and living. Moving on, VB stands for Von Bargen, my last name passed down by my late father. Legally, it's a way to distinguish myself from the generic Aesthetix brand other companies have trademarked, personally, it's a way to honor my late father and add onto his legacy. My dad was an architect for most of his life, a WW2 Navy Veteran, and tried to be a good father, even though he didn't have much to give, including time.
